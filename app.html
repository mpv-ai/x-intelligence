Thesis

Daily-driver users argue harness plus cost. Claude Code, Cursor, GLM/OpenCode, and narrow computer-use in BPO are the credible stories. Copilot and Agentforce: seats sold, usage thin. Social volume is not adoption.

Do not fund

Another general autonomous employee without a system-of-record write-path, eval, and a hard spend cap. Do not blend Gartner’s $206.5B with $7–11B standalone estimates. Annex III high-risk is mostly Dec 2027, not this month.

What’s emerging

01HappyRobot

Enterprise ops, logistics-first. $150M C, ~$1.2B, 4 Aug. Logos DHL, K+N, Uber. 5× growth and 150%+ NDR are company-stated.

02TinyFish / Mako

Mako GA 21 Jul. DoorDash 1M+ quarterly workflows (named exec). Strongest production proof. Google Hotels Japan is first-party, title-only quote.

03Zenity

Intent-aware security across Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Cursor. $125M C, 230+ staff. Fortune 1000 is an investor claim. Press totals $180 vs $185M.

04Naïve

$28.5M A, 6 Aug. CEO to TC: 30k+ developers, 10× ARR to low double-digit millions, 10 FTEs. KYC/KYB still human.

05Twin1

Out of stealth 20 Aug, $20M seed. Linklaters, Orrick, Dechert, Customers Bank. 30–50% comms automation is company-reported.

06Grok Bot

Beta 11 Aug for SuperGrok Plus/Heavy and Cursor Pro+/Ultra/Teams. Seat counts unconfirmed. Grok 4.6 same week is launch theater until people keep paying.

07Gemini Spark

3.7 Flash in 160+ countries, 13 Aug. Gemini 900M+ MAU is not Spark usage.

What users say

Harness plus cost, not which model. Failed runs still bill. Unused credits expire. Dual-source Chinese weights when US models refuse security-adjacent work.

Credible use

Claude Code for long jobs. Cursor as the editor baseline. GLM 5.3 + OpenCode as a spend-switch. Computer-use on portals (a16z: one CPG ~15–20M interactions/month). Not “agent uses my Mac.” Perplexity for cited Q&A. ElevenLabs for hero voice.

Seats ≠ use

M365 Copilot leak: ~30M paid seats, <4.5% of 450M commercial M365, 20–30% weekly use among payers. Agentforce: CIOs still not sold. Kling/Manus complaints are billing, not pixels.
